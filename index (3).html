Unsupported entity types in Australia (Business):

    Everything else on the board — Sole Trader, Partnership, Private Company, Public Company, Association & Branch, Charity/NPO, Trust (general), Co-operative, and Government Entities — is onboardable by default, subject to the notes below.

    Watch for this — general "Trust" vs specific unsupported trust structures
    Trust ≠ Superannuation Fund

    "Trust" as a general legal type is onboardable. But Superannuation Funds, SMSFs, government-managed super funds, and other regulated trusts (e.g. approved deposit funds) are all specific trust structures that fall outside our supported scope — even though "Trust" itself sits on the supported list.

    Charity / NPO — registration required
    ACNC registration is the gate

    We only onboard charities registered with the ACNC. Deregistered or unregistered charities cannot be onboarded — this is an AUP violation under section 1.2.3.d.1: "Unregistered charities are not supported from any regions." If an Association receives donations, it must also be registered with ACNC.

    Legal type correction
    Public Company Limited by Guarantee

    Not a valid "Association" legal type during onboarding. If the entity is a Public Company Limited by Guarantee, change the entity type on the profile to Public Limited instead.

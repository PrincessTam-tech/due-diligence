    Company Limited by Guarantee — conditional exception

    Supported by default. But: if the customer's own website or business description explicitly states the business is a nonprofit, do not onboard — this violates AUP 1.2.3d, even though the legal type itself sits on the supported list.

    Individual ownership must be aggregated across cross-shareholdings — a person's direct stake plus every indirect stake traced through corporate shareholders, summed together. The threshold is more than 25% (25.01% onwards), not 25% flat.

    Exception — multiple non-Singapore corporate shareholders
    When full breakdown isn't practical

    If there are multiple non-Singapore corporate shareholders, it isn't feasible or customer-friendly to request business documents for each one, and it costs unnecessary analyst time. In these cases: send the Zendesk macro 'Singapore UBO Document' (contains the UBO declaration form). If this form is used, a POI with nationality is not required.

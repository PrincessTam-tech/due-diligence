Restricted trust types (regardless of whether the trustee is an individual or a company):

    Everything else on the board — Discretionary Trading Trusts, Fixed Unit Trusts, Hybrid Trusts, Testamentary/Non-registered Trusts, and Charitable Trusts — is onboardable by default, subject to the notes below.

    Trustee eligibility follows the trust type
    Not a standalone check

    The eligibility of the trustee is tied to the type of trust they manage. We cannot onboard any trust — or trustee acting for it — that falls into a restricted category, regardless of whether that trustee is an individual or a company.

    Trustee verification path
    Individual vs corporate trustee

    Individual trustees follow sole trader verification requirements; corporate trustees follow limited company verification requirements. Either way, the underlying trust itself must still be a supported type.

    Watch for this — supported, but not handled here
    Charitable Trusts escalate to Offshore

    Charitable Trusts are an onboardable trust type — but they're escalated to the Offshore team rather than handled directly by TS analysts.
